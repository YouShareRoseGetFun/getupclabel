Amazon needs a readable barcode that matches your inventory’s required tracking method. What size does an Amazon barcode need to be?

A familiar FNSKU sheet format is 1 × 2⅝ inches, with 30 labels on US Letter paper. Amazon lists this format in its Amazon barcode label requirements. It matches the Avery 5160 layout supported by this site’s UPC PDF export, although that export creates UPC labels, not Amazon FNSKU labels.

Label dimensions describe the sticker, not the minimum size of the bars printed inside it. The official guidance does not establish a universal minimum barcode rectangle that you can safely apply to every downloaded label. Keep the Seller Central artwork at its supplied proportions instead of inventing a smaller version.

The quiet zones are the clear white areas beside the barcode. Cropping them can make an otherwise sharp symbol unreadable. Amazon specifies white space of 0.25 inch at the sides and 0.125 inch above and below the barcode and related text; see its label formatting requirements.

Before buying stock, compare the template selected in Seller Central with the package of labels. Then inspect a printed sample for clipping at the sheet edges. If the design does not fit, correct the paper or template setting rather than squeezing the bars into the available space.

Check the complete printed label, including its readable identifier and product details. A successful scan alone does not tell you that the label belongs on the item you are preparing.

FNSKU vs UPC: which barcode goes on the label?

An FNSKU is Amazon’s fulfillment identifier for the relevant inventory offering. A UPC identifies a trade item through the GS1 system and can travel across retail channels. See the GS1 US explanation of GTINs for the distinction between product identifiers and the barcode carrying them.

When your inventory requires an Amazon barcode, apply the correct FNSKU label over the original scannable product barcode. Do not leave the UPC and FNSKU exposed side by side for receiving staff to choose between. Amazon’s product labeling guide explains covering the manufacturer barcode.

That does not mean every FBA unit universally needs an FNSKU sticker. Amazon’s current FBA barcode requirements distinguish eligible brand owners using manufacturer barcodes from resellers required to use Amazon barcodes. Review the applicable marketplace and your offering’s settings before preparing stock.

If you need artwork for a legitimately assigned UPC, use the UPC label generator. It cannot turn a UPC into an FNSKU or establish your eligibility for manufacturer barcode tracking. Keep your source number records separate from downloaded label files.

Before labeling a mixed shipment, match each product variation to its offering. A clear label for the wrong item is still an identification error. Check the product name and condition before applying each batch.

What the X00 prefix means

You may recognize Amazon labels by an X00 prefix followed by letters and numbers. Amazon’s FBA setup guide illustrates this seller inventory identifier. The prefix is a useful clue, not proof that a label matches your product or a rule for constructing your own identifier.

FNSKU artwork uses Code 128 rather than UPC-A. Amazon describes the encoding in its FBA barcode guidance. The distinction matters because an alphanumeric fulfillment identifier cannot be entered into a numeric UPC generator and retain its meaning.

Obtain the FNSKU and its printable label through your Amazon seller account. This page explains the format and printing process; it does not issue identifiers. Recreating an identifier from a photograph or guessing the next characters is not a substitute for the assigned label.

When you export labels, save them with a descriptive filename and check the associated product before printing. If you change an offering or its barcode settings, verify the current label in Seller Central again. Do not assume a previously saved file is correct simply because its prefix looks familiar. Compare the readable identifier with the current account record before proceeding.

Print settings that actually scan

Use a printer capable of at least 300 DPI, as specified in Amazon’s printer requirements. Higher resolution helps preserve bar edges, but it cannot fix the wrong identifier, an incorrect template, or artwork that was already blurred before printing.

Print at 100% or Actual size, with Fit to page disabled. Amazon’s label printing guide instructs you to print without scaling. Check both the document viewer and printer driver because either can change the output size.

A laser printer uses toner on compatible sheet labels. A direct thermal printer uses heat-sensitive stock that can darken under heat and suffer surface damage from abrasion. Thermal transfer printers instead use a ribbon; choose the media for your actual printer type, not just the dimensions printed on its box.

For sheet printing, use Avery 5160 or compatible stock matching the Amazon template described above. For thermal printing, choose the appropriate roll-label output in Seller Central. Do not feed office label sheets into a device designed for rolls.

Our UPC label generator provides separate manufacturer-barcode artwork; download FNSKU artwork from Amazon. Keep those print jobs separate so a saved UPC sheet is not mistaken for an Amazon label batch.

Print a sample, inspect the bars, and scan it after applying it to the actual package. Keep the successful paper and driver settings with your packing instructions.

Five reasons Amazon rejects a barcode

These are practical failure checks, not a guarantee that every affected unit will receive the same rejection code. Inspect the finished package before sealing your shipment.

  • Bars are too small or clipped. Shrinking a label can merge narrow spaces, while an incorrect template can cut off the outer bars. Return to the original label file and match its paper setting. Inspect the edge labels on the sheet before printing the remaining batch.
  • Quiet zones are missing. A tightly cropped image or nearby package graphics can remove the clear area a scanner needs. Keep the supplied white border intact and place the whole label on an uncluttered surface. Do not add decorative borders or trim the sticker directly against the printed bars.
  • The surface reflects light. Glossy film, reflective tape, or a curved package can make a barcode difficult to read from ordinary scanning angles. Prefer a flat placement and avoid adding reflective material over the symbol. Test the applied label rather than assuming the bare sticker behaves the same after wrapping.
  • Print contrast is weak. Faded toner, unsuitable media, or an incorrect thermal setting can leave broken bars and gray backgrounds. Check the stock, clean the printer as directed by its manufacturer, and reprint damaged output. Raising print darkness without inspection can also spread bars and close the spaces between them.
  • Placement obscures the symbol. A skewed sticker crossing a seam or fold can wrinkle or hide the barcode. Apply it smoothly on an accessible flat area and check that the entire symbol remains visible. Cover competing product barcodes when an Amazon label is required, without folding the new barcode around an edge.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print FNSKU labels on a regular printer?

Yes, a suitable laser printer can print Seller Central sheet labels on compatible stock. Match the downloaded template to the paper and inspect a sample before labeling inventory. Amazon recommends thermal or laser printing in its barcode requirements. Use the original label file and confirm that the printer has not resized it.

Does Amazon accept a UPC barcode instead of FNSKU?

Sometimes. Eligible brand owners may use manufacturer barcodes, while resellers must follow Amazon barcode requirements. Check the current rules for your marketplace and the barcode settings for your offering. If an Amazon barcode is required, a newly drawn UPC does not replace it, even when that UPC scans successfully.

What resolution should I print barcode labels at?

Amazon specifies at least 300 DPI in its printer requirements. Select a suitable printer and start with the original Seller Central label file. Increasing the resolution of a blurry screenshot does not restore missing bar detail. Check the printed result for sharp edges, clear spaces, and complete product information before using the batch.

Why does my barcode scan on screen but not on paper?

A display and a printed surface behave differently. Printing can introduce scaling, fading, smudges, reflections, or clipped margins that were absent on screen. Check the output settings and media, then scan the label after applying it to your package. A screen scan is useful, but it does not validate the finished label.

Can I generate UPC barcodes for free on this site?

Yes. This site generates UPC artwork for numbers you are authorized to use and checks the final check digit. It does not license product numbers, register products, or create Amazon FNSKUs. Use Seller Central for Amazon labels, and confirm which barcode your inventory requires before choosing your artwork.
