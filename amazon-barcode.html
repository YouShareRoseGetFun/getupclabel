Amazon Barcode Size and Requirements

Amazon needs a readable barcode that matches your inventory’s required tracking method. For an Amazon barcode, print the FNSKU label supplied by Seller Central and preserve its layout, white margins, and product information. Choose the matching label template rather than shrinking the artwork to fit spare stock. A manufacturer UPC can be appropriate for eligible inventory, so confirm your barcode choice before covering existing packaging. This guide explains label dimensions, printing, and placement for individual units. It does not generate FNSKUs or replace the instructions shown for your product in Seller Central.

What size does an Amazon barcode need to be?

A familiar FNSKU sheet format is 2⅝ × 1 inches (width × height), with 30 labels on US Letter paper. Amazon lists this sheet layout in its public FBA quick-start labeling guide (PDF). It matches the Avery 5160 layout supported by this site’s UPC PDF export, although that export creates UPC labels, not Amazon FNSKU labels.

Label dimensions describe the sticker, not the minimum size of the bars printed inside it. A sticker size does not establish a minimum size for the barcode symbol. We have not verified a current minimum symbol dimension in the signed-in Seller Central instructions, so this page does not state one. Keep the Seller Central artwork at its supplied proportions instead of inventing a smaller version.

The quiet zones are the clear white areas beside the barcode. Cropping them can make an otherwise sharp symbol unreadable. Preserve the full white margins in the Seller Central label file. We have not verified the current numerical margin requirements inside a signed-in seller account; check that account guidance before resizing or recreating a label.

Place the item label on a flat, externally accessible surface of the unit’s final packaging. Avoid folds, seams, and sealing tape across the bars. If you add a bag or other preparation layer, make sure the required item barcode can be scanned without opening it.

FNSKU vs UPC: which barcode goes on the label?

An FNSKU is Amazon’s fulfillment identifier for the relevant inventory offering. A UPC identifies a trade item through the GS1 system and can travel across retail channels. See the GS1 US explanation of GTINs for the distinction between product identifiers and the barcode carrying them.

When your inventory requires an Amazon barcode, apply the correct FNSKU label over the original scannable product barcode. Do not leave the UPC and FNSKU exposed side by side for receiving staff to choose between. The public labeling guide linked above explains covering the manufacturer barcode.

That does not mean every FBA unit universally needs an FNSKU sticker. Amazon distinguishes eligible brand owners using manufacturer barcodes from resellers required to use Amazon barcodes. Review the applicable marketplace and your offering’s settings before preparing stock.

If you need artwork for a legitimately assigned UPC, use the UPC label generator. It cannot turn a UPC into an FNSKU or establish your eligibility for manufacturer barcode tracking.

For a mixed-SKU shipment using Amazon barcodes, apply the corresponding FNSKU to each sellable unit before packing. A shipment label on the outer carton does not replace the item labels inside it. Separate the products while labeling so units from different offerings do not receive the same sticker.

What the X00 prefix means

You may recognize Amazon labels by an X00 prefix followed by letters and numbers. Use the identifier assigned in your seller account, rather than treating the prefix as a template. The prefix is a useful clue, not proof that a label matches your product or a rule for constructing your own identifier.

FNSKU artwork uses Code 128 rather than UPC-A. Use the barcode artwork supplied by Amazon rather than selecting a UPC font for the same characters. The distinction matters because an alphanumeric fulfillment identifier cannot be entered into a numeric UPC generator and retain its meaning.

Print settings that actually scan

Use a printer capable of at least 300 DPI, as specified in the Seller Central label and printer requirements (seller sign-in required). Confirm the current specifications in your account before printing. Higher resolution helps preserve bar edges, but it cannot fix the wrong identifier, an incorrect template, or artwork that was already blurred before printing.

Print at 100% or Actual size, with Fit to page disabled. The public Amazon labeling PDF linked above instructs you to print without scaling. Check both the document viewer and printer driver because either can change the output size.

A laser printer uses toner on compatible sheet labels. Direct thermal stock is sensitive to heat and abrasion, and its image can fade over time. Thermal transfer printers use a compatible ribbon and stock; Zebra’s thermal printing overview explains the difference. For laser printing, buy labels explicitly rated for laser printers; unsuitable media can cause poor toner adhesion or feeding problems.

If your Avery 5160 output is misaligned, select Letter paper in both the viewer and printer driver, and keep Actual size selected. An A4 default can cause scaling or clipping, including at the rightmost column, depending on the driver. Test on plain paper against the label sheet before using adhesive stock; do not compensate by shrinking the barcode.

For thermal printing, choose the matching roll-label output in Seller Central. Do not feed office label sheets into a device designed for rolls, or print the full Letter sheet PDF onto a small roll label.

Our UPC label generator provides separate manufacturer-barcode artwork; download FNSKU artwork from Amazon. Keep those print jobs separate so a saved UPC sheet is not mistaken for an Amazon label batch.

Five reasons Amazon rejects a barcode

  • Bars are too small or clipped. Shrinking a label can merge narrow spaces, while an incorrect template can cut off the outer bars. Return to the original label file and match its paper setting. Inspect the edge labels on the sheet before printing the remaining batch.
  • Quiet zones are missing. A tightly cropped image or nearby package graphics can remove the clear area a scanner needs. Keep the supplied white border intact and place the whole label on an uncluttered surface. Do not add decorative borders or trim the sticker directly against the printed bars.
  • The surface reflects light. Glossy film, reflective tape, or a curved package can make a barcode difficult to read from ordinary scanning angles. Prefer a flat placement and avoid adding reflective material over the symbol. Test the applied label rather than assuming the bare sticker behaves the same after wrapping.
  • Print contrast is weak. Faded toner, unsuitable media, or an incorrect thermal setting can leave broken bars and gray backgrounds. Check the stock, clean the printer as directed by its manufacturer, and reprint damaged output. Raising print darkness without inspection can also spread bars and close the spaces between them.
  • Placement obscures the symbol. A skewed sticker crossing a seam or fold can wrinkle or hide the barcode. Apply it smoothly on an accessible flat area and check that the entire symbol remains visible. Cover competing product barcodes when an Amazon label is required, without folding the new barcode around an edge.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print FNSKU labels on a regular printer?

Yes, a suitable laser printer can print Seller Central sheet labels on compatible stock. Match the downloaded template to the paper and inspect a sample before labeling inventory. Amazon recommends thermal or laser printing in its barcode requirements. Use the original label file and confirm that the printer has not resized it.

Does Amazon accept a UPC barcode instead of FNSKU?

Sometimes. Eligible brand owners may use manufacturer barcodes, while resellers must follow Amazon barcode requirements. Check the current rules for your marketplace and the barcode settings for your offering. If an Amazon barcode is required, a newly drawn UPC does not replace it, even when that UPC scans successfully.

What resolution should I print barcode labels at?

Amazon specifies at least 300 DPI in its printer requirements. Select a suitable printer and start with the original Seller Central label file. Increasing the resolution of a blurry screenshot does not restore missing bar detail. Check the printed result for sharp edges, clear spaces, and complete product information before using the batch.

Why does my barcode scan on screen but not on paper?

A display and a printed surface behave differently. Printing can introduce scaling, fading, smudges, reflections, or clipped margins that were absent on screen. Check the output settings and media, then scan the label after applying it to your package. A screen scan is useful, but it does not validate the finished label.
