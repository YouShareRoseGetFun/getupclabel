Contact & Support

For bug reports and support, open an issue on the project repository. A GitHub account may be required. Do not include confidential product data in public reports.

Include your browser and operating system, selected label template, a non-confidential example UPC, the steps you followed, and what happened. For printing issues, include the printer model, paper size, and scaling setting.

For number licensing or ownership questions, contact your local GS1 organisation. This tool cannot assign numbers or change retailer product records.
